About Me

Hello! My name is Jayne Sobczyk-Moore.

I am working toward a new career in information science and librarianship, and pursuing a Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) degree at Simmons University. I am particularly interested in special librarianship, medical and biomedical information, research data management, metadata, and information technology.

My background in laboratory science has given me experience working with scientific information, research data, data visualization, and complex clinical concepts. I am interested in bringing these strengths into the information profession, particularly in roles that support researchers, clinicians, scientists, and other specialized information users.

As I continue my MLIS education, I am developing skills in metadata standards, information organization, digital information systems, web technologies, and research data management. I am especially interested in the intersection of science, technology, data, and information, and in finding ways to make complex information more organized, accessible, discoverable, and useful.
